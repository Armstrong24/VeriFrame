What the model relies on

Architecture

  1. Video: 8 uniformly sampled frames → CLIP ViT-B/32 embeddings, plus editing statistics (shot cuts, motion, duration, resolution, sharpness).
  2. Text: caption → CLIP text embedding + MiniLM sentence embedding + style features (hashtags, caps, clickbait words, emojis).
  3. Cross-modal: per-frame caption-frame CLIP similarity, temporal embedding drift, element-wise text×video interaction.
  4. Models: Logistic Regression, RBF-SVM, LightGBM, XGBoost and a Cross-Modal Attention Fusion Transformer (text↔frame co-attention, 5-seed average).
  5. Ensemble: weighted soft-vote; weights and decision threshold tuned on the validation split for macro-F1.
VeriFrame · research prototype · predictions are probabilistic and should not be treated as fact-checks.
